HOW TO READ THE NUMBERS

Financial terms, made simple

01

Revenue

The money a business earns from selling goods or services before expenses are deducted. Growing revenue can indicate expanding sales, but does not guarantee profit.

02

Net income

What remains after reported expenses. Positive net income indicates a profit for the reporting period; negative net income indicates a loss.

03

Profit margin

The percentage of revenue left as net income. It helps compare profitability, particularly between periods in the same company.

04

P/E ratio

Price divided by earnings per share. A high ratio may reflect growth expectations or a high valuation; a low ratio is not automatically a bargain.
