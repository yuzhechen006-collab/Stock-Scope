LEARN AS YOU GO

What do these numbers mean?

01

Daily return

How much the closing price changed compared with the previous trading day, expressed as a percentage.

02

Volatility

How much daily returns vary. Higher volatility means larger fluctuations, not necessarily better or worse performance.

03

Annualised volatility

A common estimate that scales daily volatility by the square root of 252 trading days. It is not a forecast of returns.

04

Drawdown and moving average

Drawdown is the fall from a previous high. The dashed 20-day average smooths daily noise so the underlying trend is easier to see.
